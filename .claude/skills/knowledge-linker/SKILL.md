# Knowledge Linker

1. Read the target note and its existing links.
2. Search for related notes by concept, not just matching keywords.
3. Suggest relationships such as prerequisite, implementation, example, alternative, trade-off, or experience.
4. Add a short explanation under `Related` when the relationship is not obvious.
5. Do not create placeholder notes or mass-link every matching word.
6. Report broken links and possible duplicate concepts.
7. Present exact edits and wait for approval before applying them.
